# HealGym

**Your fitness journey, made personal.**

HealGym is a fitness software platform designed to help you build a training routine that fits your goals, experience, and lifestyle. It brings personalized workouts, progress tracking, and a focus on healthy habits together in one place.

## What HealGym Offers

- **Personalized Training** — Workouts tailored to your goals, experience, and availability.
- **Progress Tracking** — Follow your training journey and see how you improve over time.
- **Individual Profile** — A fitness experience adapted to your needs and routine.
- **Diet & Wellness** — A broader focus on healthy eating habits and the balance between training and nutrition.

Whether you're getting started or looking to improve your consistency, HealGym helps you stay focused on your fitness goals.
